GravitySlingshot

Bend your shot around planets. Hit the green ring.

How to play

  1. Press on the blue launcher and pull back — like a slingshot.
  2. Pull further for more power. The dots show the start of your path only.
  3. Release to fire. Gravity does the rest.
  • Planets pull you in
  • Black holes pull hard — don't cross the orange line
  • Repellers push you away
  • Stars: +50 if collected on the winning shot

5 shots per level. Fewer shots = more points. Faint lines show your earlier tries.
Planet masses vary slightly each run, so the dots are a guide, not a promise.
